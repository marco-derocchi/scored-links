Scored Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scored Labs collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Scored mobile app.

Information We Process

  • Bluetooth device data needed to discover and connect to your scoreboard hardware.
  • Camera access used to scan QR codes for pairing and setup.
  • Basic app and connection diagnostics used to improve reliability and troubleshoot issues.

How We Use Information

  • To pair your phone with compatible scoreboard devices.
  • To process setup links or tokens for device onboarding.
  • To keep scoring and synchronization features functioning correctly.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with service providers required to run core app functionality, such as secure backend infrastructure.

Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary to provide app functionality, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your Choices

  • You can stop Bluetooth and camera access by disabling permissions in your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

Children

Scored is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Scored Labs at info@scoredlabs.com.
